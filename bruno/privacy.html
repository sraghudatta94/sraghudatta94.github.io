Bruno · Privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026.

Bruno is a personal app used only by its owner. Nobody else can sign in.

What Bruno uses

  • Google sign-in: your Google account's email address and name, only to confirm that it is the owner signing in.
  • Google Drive (drive.file): Bruno creates its own folder and files in the owner's Drive (call recordings) and can read only the files it created. It cannot see any other file in the Drive.

Where data is kept

The owner's data stays in the owner's own accounts: the owner's database and the owner's Google Drive. Bruno does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone, and shows no ads.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
